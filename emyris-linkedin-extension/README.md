# Emyris LinkedIn Live Sync Extension (Chrome Manifest V3)

A lightweight Chrome Extension that connects your active, authenticated LinkedIn browser session directly with the **Emyris Executive Desk** (`https://emyrishr.in/admin?tab=linkedin`).

### What it does:
1. **Reads Live Feeds directly from your logged-in LinkedIn tab** (author, role/headline, post content, and exact `data-urn` / permalinks).
2. **Generates exact individual post permalinks** (`https://www.linkedin.com/feed/update/urn:li:activity:.../`) so clicking "Open Post" takes you directly to that specific post.
3. **Pushes live feeds directly to your Emyris Portal** with executive AI replies personalized for **J. Ranjan Dash** (Business Operation Head, Emyris Biolifesciences).

### How to Install in Google Chrome:
1. Open Google Chrome.
2. In the URL bar, go to `chrome://extensions/`.
3. In the top right corner, toggle **Developer mode** to **ON**.
4. Click **Load unpacked** (top left).
5. Select the folder:
   `D:\MY WORK FLOW\Emyris Onboard App\emyris-linkedin-extension`
6. Done! The blue/purple "EM" icon will appear in your Chrome toolbar.

### How to Use:
- Keep your LinkedIn tab (`https://www.linkedin.com/feed/`) open in Chrome.
- In `https://emyrishr.in/admin?tab=linkedin`, click **Sync Live Feeds**.
- The page will instantly pull the live posts from your open LinkedIn tab!
